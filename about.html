About Me

My current learning and practical focus includes manual testing, functional testing, non-functional testing, regression testing, test case design, test planning, defect reporting, bug tracking, SDLC, STLC, verification and validation, requirements analysis and test environment setup.

I enjoy looking at software from a user's perspective, finding edge cases, checking whether requirements are correctly implemented and documenting issues clearly so developers can reproduce and fix them. I am also developing my technical foundation in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Git/GitHub and software development concepts, with the goal of moving toward automation testing and modern QA tools.

My portfolio includes academic and practice projects such as a Health & Fitness App, Employee Management System, Tic Tac Toe Game, University Management System and E-Commerce App. These projects help me understand different application workflows and practice thinking about quality across real-world features.
